## Night-loop for Android 0.8.2

- **Brightness range % instead of the dimmer or brighter box.** Wait for picture, Swipe and scan, Walk to and Image visible conditions now have a number for how much dimmer or brighter than the captured picture a match may look. Blank is 6% (as captured, with room for screen noise), 0 is exactly as captured, 30 lets a copy 30% dimmer or brighter match. Steps that had the box ticked read as 50%.
- **Chips stay where you put them.** Wait, key and other step chips that aren't taps could come back in their old spot when the play bar opened again. Their spot is now kept while you drag, and again whenever the macro saves or the play bar closes.
