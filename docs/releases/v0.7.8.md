## Night-loop for Android 0.7.8

- **Type Infinite.** Every box where blank means Infinite (wait times, "Give up after", Loop times, Max tries, Repeat the macro) now also takes the word **Infinite**, so the full keyboard opens for them. Blank still means Infinite too.
- **Waits show when they give up.** A Wait for picture, text or color that isn't Infinite now says so on its step, like "gives up after 10 s". Waits made before 0.7.3 gave up after 10 seconds and then went on, which looked like they didn't wait for the picture. Open the step and type Infinite (or clear the box) to make it wait until the picture shows.
