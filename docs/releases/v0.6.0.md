## Night-loop for Android 0.6.0

- **Wait for text, pictures and colors.** New Screen blocks: Wait for text, Wait for picture and Wait for color, each with an "until it's gone" version. Pick the picture or color right on the screen.
- **Picture settings.** Match colors on or off, confidence, size tolerance and color tolerance, like on PC.
- **If / Repeat until can check the screen.** Conditions for text on screen, a picture on screen and a pixel color, plus NOT.
- **Wait after taps / Wait after every block.** New settings under Settings › Playing, like the PC's Wait after clicks.
- Macros that read the screen ask for Android's screen capture once per session ("Start now").
