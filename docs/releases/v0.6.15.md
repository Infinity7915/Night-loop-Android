## Night-loop for Android 0.6.15

- **No more bursts of taps.** Taps keep a short gap (40 ms) between them, and the play bar only shows the newest step instead of catching up through many dots at once.
- **The ☰ step list stays open while a macro plays**, and taps under it reach the app below, like the play bar.
- **New setting: Follow the playing step** (Settings › Playing, on by default). With the ☰ list open, the step being played is highlighted green and kept in the middle of the list.
