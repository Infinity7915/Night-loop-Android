## Night-loop for Android 0.8.0

- **Dimmer or brighter.** Picture steps (Wait for picture, Swipe and scan, Walk to picture, and "Picture is on screen" checks) have a new box under Match colors: **Also match dimmer or brighter copies**. Off (the default), a picture is only found in the colors you captured, so a dimmer copy isn't tapped. On, the screen's colors are averaged to the picture's brightness first, so dimmer or brighter copies match too, like before this version.
- **Pick where to tap.** Wait for picture, Swipe and scan and Walk to picture have a new **Tap spot**. Show the picture on screen, press "Pick tap spot on screen" and touch anywhere: on the picture or beside it. The spot is saved relative to the picture, so it follows the picture wherever it is found (and grows or shrinks with it). "Middle" puts it back in the middle.
