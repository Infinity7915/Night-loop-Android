## Night-loop for Android 0.8.5

- **Big pictures match at their real size.** A picked area wider than 200 px is shrunk before it's saved, so Wait for picture with Size tolerance 0 could never match it at full size, and a poor look-alike (even a loading or plain screen) could pass. Pictures now remember how much they were shrunk and are looked for at their real size. **Pick your pictures again once** after updating so they carry this.
