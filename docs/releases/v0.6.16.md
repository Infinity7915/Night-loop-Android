## Night-loop for Android 0.6.16

- **Seasonal themes** (Settings › Playing, off until you turn it on). Around holidays, Night-loop's home-screen icon and bubble dress up for the season:
  - Christmas week: the moon at the North Pole, with northern lights, snow, an igloo and a Santa hat
  - New Year: fireworks and a party hat
  - Valentine's: hearts and Cupid's arrow
  - St. Patrick's: a rainbow and a pot of gold
  - Easter: bunny ears and eggs
  - 4th of July: fireworks and a flag
  - Halloween: a witch hat, a jack-o'-lantern and a ghost
  - Thanksgiving week (US in November, Canada in October): falling leaves and a pie
  - Lunar New Year: red lanterns, a pagoda and a red envelope
  - Mid-Autumn Festival: a full harvest moon with the jade rabbit, floating lanterns and mooncakes
- **Holidays follow your country.** Night-loop reads your phone's region setting (it doesn't use GPS and saves nothing). China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Singapore, Malaysia, Korea and Vietnam get Lunar New Year and Mid-Autumn. The US gets the 4th of July and Thanksgiving. Canada gets its own Thanksgiving week.
- The home-screen icon may move once after this update, because of how Android swaps icons. If it disappears from your home screen, add it again from the app list.
