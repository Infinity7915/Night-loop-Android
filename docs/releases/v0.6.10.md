## Night-loop for Android 0.6.10

- **Fix: the first tap of a macro played from the bubble didn't land** (you had to tap twice). The step dots now let touches through before the first tap, not right after it.
- **Clearer message when the accessibility switch is on but taps still can't be sent.** Android sometimes stops Night-loop's accessibility service (often after an update) while the switch still shows on. Night-loop now tells you to turn it off and on again.
