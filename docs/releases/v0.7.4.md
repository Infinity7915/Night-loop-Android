## Night-loop for Android 0.7.4

- **Tap a step to select it, hold it to open its settings.** In the play bar editor, a quick tap now only highlights a dot or chip, so ⊕, + and ⏱ add the next step right after it and ↑ ↓ move it. Hold the step for half a second to open its settings, like before.
- **Taps don't get in each other's way.** Only one tap, swipe or hold runs at a time, even with several macros playing, so one can't cut off another. Taps also go through Night-loop's pop-up windows from the play bar, like they already did for the bubble, the play bar and the steps.
