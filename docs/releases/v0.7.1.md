## Night-loop for Android 0.7.1

- **Fixed: Wait for picture, text and color went on right away.**
  - The wait now only counts something as found after two looks in a row agree, so a stale screen or a flicker can't end it early.
  - When you pick a picture or a color, Night-loop now hides its own dots, chips and play bar first. Before, a picture could include one of Night-loop's own steps, and that step was always "found".
  - "Give up after" is now in **seconds**. The old box was in milliseconds, so typing 10 meant 10 ms, and the wait gave up at once. Leave it blank for Infinite: blank really means Infinite now (it used to fall back to 10 seconds).
- **Tap when found.** Wait for text, Wait for picture and Wait for color have a "When found" choice: tap it, then go on.
- **New block: Swipe and scan.** It swipes (for example to turn the camera) again and again until a picture or text shows up, then can tap it.
- **New block: Walk to picture.** It holds the joystick and walks toward a picture or text, steering at it, until it's near the middle of the screen. You can also have it stop walking when a check is true. It can tap it when it gets there.
- **Step chips stay where you put them.** Wait, key and other chips in the bubble come back where you left them. Turn on Settings › Playing › Arrange steps automatically to line them up in columns instead.
- **Fixed: past about 30 steps, new chips landed off the edge of the screen.** They now wrap back onto the screen.
- **The bubble's step count explains itself.** The app lists each wait after a tap as its own block, so its count is higher. The bubble now shows both numbers.
