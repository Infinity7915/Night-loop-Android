A Back button in the macro editor.

## What's new

- **← Back** at the top of the macro editor takes you to the home page. Your changes are kept as a draft like before; tap Save so the bubble plays them.

## Installing

1. Uninstall the older Night-loop first (this preview still uses a temporary signing key).
2. Download `Night-loop-0.4.1.apk` below on your phone and open it.
3. Turn the Night-loop accessibility service on again in Set up.
