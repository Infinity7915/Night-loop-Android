## Night-loop for Android 0.6.8

- **All the If / Repeat until checks from the PC version.** Pick "App is not open", "Text is not on screen", "Picture is not on screen" or "Spot is not that color" straight from the list, next to the normal checks.
- **Fix: taking a picture or picking a color could close Night-loop.** If Android refuses screen capture, Night-loop now says so instead of closing, and a finished screen capture no longer switches off the new one.
