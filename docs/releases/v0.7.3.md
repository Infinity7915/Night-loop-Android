## Night-loop for Android 0.7.3

- **Wait blocks wait until it's found.** New Wait for text, Wait for picture and Wait for color blocks (and their "gone" versions) start with "Give up after" blank, which means Infinite. Before, they gave up after 10 seconds (5 for colors) and went on, which looked like the wait didn't wait. Blocks you already made keep their time: clear the box to make one Infinite.
