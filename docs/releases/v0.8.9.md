## Night-loop for Android 0.8.9

- **Night-loop's copy of the screen no longer freezes.** After a few looks at the screen, Night-loop kept seeing the same old picture of it until Android tidied up on its own. That one fault caused several things:
  - A picture picked over a pop-up that had just opened (or just closed) was taken from the screen as it was moments before, so the step's settings showed what was underneath, at the same spot.
  - Steps like Wait for picture looked at that old screen. If the picture was in it, they went on at once, so a long macro ran through its steps much faster than the game, then ended. If it wasn't, they waited for nothing.
  - Match colors could look as if it failed, because it compared the picture with an old screen.
  Every look now sees the screen as it is right then.
- **Pictures are never taken again when a step taps.** The saved picture only changes when you pick it again.
- **Quick-action buttons.** In the bubble's list, each macro has a "Quick action" switch. Turn it on and a ⚡ button with the macro's name stays on screen over the game: tap it to play the macro, tap it again (it shows ■) to stop. Drag it anywhere; it comes back where you left it.
- **Changes the server refuses are no longer thrown away.** Closing the bubble's steps with ✓ said "Kept as a draft" even when the server had refused the change (the macro over 200 KB, the daily block limit, a step with a problem). The steps now stay on screen and say why.
- **A picture too detailed to save is refused when you pick it**, with a message to drag a smaller box, instead of the whole macro failing to save.
- **The editor in the app shows the newest copy.** Coming back to it after changing the macro from the bubble loads the bubble's changes, so its older copy can't put an old picture back.
