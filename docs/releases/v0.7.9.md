## Night-loop for Android 0.7.9

- **Picture blocks look at the screen as it is now.** Night-loop could look at an old copy of the screen, from when the macro was being set up. A Wait for picture then "found" the picture right away, for example while the game was still loading, and tapped where it used to be. Night-loop now keeps its copy of the screen up to date the whole time.
- **Whole screen only.** On Android 14 and newer, the screen sharing prompt now only offers the entire screen. Sharing one app hid the game whenever another app was in front.
- **Double tap to change a step.** In the play bar, one tap on a step (a dot, a chip or a row in ☰ Steps in order) only picks it, so ↑ ↓ and new steps use it. Double tap a step to open its settings. Holding a dot or chip still opens them too.
