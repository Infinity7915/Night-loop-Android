## Night-loop for Android 0.6.2

- **Delete account** in Settings › Account, like on PC. Confirm with your password; you have 30 days to change your mind by contacting support.
- **New Set up step: Allow restricted settings** (Android 13+). Its Open button goes straight to Night-loop's App info, where the 3 dots at the top right have "Allow restricted settings". Before, Android blocked the accessibility switch with no way forward from Night-loop.
- **Notifications' Open button** now goes to Night-loop's notification settings once Android stops asking.
- **Smaller download**: the build for old 32-bit x86 emulators is dropped.
