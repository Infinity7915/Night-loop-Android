## Night-loop for Android 0.8.8

- **A grayed-out copy is no longer taken for the colored picture.** A grayed-out button or card has the same shape as the colored one, and it can be just as bright, so Match colors let it through: on pictures with many or pale colors even at 100% Confidence, and with Average colors on for most pictures. Wait for picture then tapped it anyway. Match colors now also checks how much of the picture's color is still there. A copy that has lost more than about a third of it doesn't count as found (more than about half with Average colors or a wide Brightness range, since game lighting washes colors out a little).
- **"Test on screen now" on steps that look at the screen.** Open a step like Wait for picture and press it while the game shows what you want to check. Night-loop looks once, the way the macro does while it plays, and says what it saw. For a picture that is how alike the best spot is, and whether it was found, the wrong size, or the right shape in the wrong colors (a grayed-out copy). Nothing is tapped.
- **Pictures picked from the editor inside the app are taken from the game.** From that editor (not the bubble), Night-loop came back in front before the picture, color or tap spot was taken, so it could be taken from Night-loop's own screen coming back instead of from the game. Night-loop now comes back only after it has looked.
- **The saved picture is shown in color**, so you can see the colors Match colors compares. If one looks wrong, pick it again.
- **What stays the same.** A picture with hardly any color (white text, a gray icon) is matched as before, since there is no color to lose. With Match colors off only the shape counts, so a grayed-out copy still matches there.
- **See-through buttons.** If the picture is mostly the game showing through a see-through button, its colors are the game's. With Average colors on, such a picture picked over something colorful is no longer found over something gray. Turn Match colors off for those.
