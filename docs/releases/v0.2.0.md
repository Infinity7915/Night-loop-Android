Night-loop for Android can now make and play macros.

## What's new

- **Sign in** with your Night-loop account, including the emailed code. Tick "Trust this phone" to skip the code next time. One device is signed in at a time, so signing in on the phone signs Night-loop out on your PC (and the other way round).
- **Your macros**: Android macros are listed first with a ▶ Play / ■ Stop button. PC macros are shown as "PC only". The PC app shows Android macros with an "Android" tag and won't open or play them.
- **Editor**: tap New macro, then Add block. Tap a block to change it, use ↑ ↓ to move it, and hold it to add a block below, duplicate, turn off or delete it. Changes autosave as a draft; Save makes them the copy that plays. You can set how many times the macro repeats, or leave it blank for Infinite.
- **Blocks**: Tap, Long press, Swipe, Phone button (Back, Home, Recent apps, Notifications), Open app, Wait for app, Wait, Random wait, Loop, If / Else, Repeat until "app is (not) in front", Label, Go to, Stop, Notify, Play sound, Run macro, Pause / Resume macro, Comment and Description.
- **Pick on screen**: in a Tap, Long press or Swipe block, Night-loop steps aside so you can touch the exact spot in your game.
- **Bubble panel**: tap the bubble to see your Android macros and play or stop them over any app. The bubble gets a green ring while a macro plays.

## Installing

1. **Uninstall Night-loop 0.1.0 first.** Previews are signed with a temporary key, so Android won't update over them.
2. Download `Night-loop-0.2.0.apk` below on your phone and open it.
3. Open Night-loop, sign in, and turn on anything listed under **Set up**.
4. On Android 13 or newer, if the accessibility switch says it's restricted: Settings › Apps › Night-loop › ⋮ › **Allow restricted settings**, then try again.

## Good to know

- Finding pictures or text on the screen isn't on Android yet; it's the next big step.
- Needs Android 8.0 or newer.
