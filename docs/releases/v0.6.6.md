## Night-loop for Android 0.6.6

- **Fix: Night-loop could stop when the bubble was turned on** on some phones (Android 10–13). The bubble's background service now tells Android it only captures the screen after you allow it.
- **Crash reports.** If Night-loop closes unexpectedly, the next time you open it a card lets you copy a short report (the error, Night-loop version and phone model) to send to us.
