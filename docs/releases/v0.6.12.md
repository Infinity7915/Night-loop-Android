## Night-loop for Android 0.6.12

- **Taps go through Night-loop's own windows.** If the play bar, the bubble or its panel sits over a tap or swipe spot, that window lets the touch through for that moment, so the app underneath gets it.
- **Type text speed.** Type text blocks have "Wait between letters (ms)", in the app editor and in the bubble's ⌨ card (0 types it all at once).
- **Stacked steps don't tap each other.** A tap or swipe that lands on another step's dot or Wait chip goes to the app underneath, and that step still plays at its own turn.
