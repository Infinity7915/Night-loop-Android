First preview of Night-loop for Android.

## What's in it

- **Set up**: the three things Night-loop needs on Android (Display over other apps, the Night-loop accessibility service, Notifications), each with an On/Off status and a button that opens the right setting.
- **Floating bubble**: a Night-loop bubble that stays on top of other apps and the home screen. Drag it anywhere, tap it to come back to Night-loop. Stop it from the "Night-loop is running" notification or the Hide bubble button.
- **Server check**: shows whether Night-loop can reach api.night-loop.win.

Signing in and playing macros from the bubble come in the next update.

## Installing

1. Download `Night-loop-0.1.0.apk` below on your phone and open it.
2. Allow installing from your browser or file app when Android asks.
3. Open Night-loop and turn on each item under **Set up**.
4. On Android 13 or newer, if the accessibility switch says it's restricted: Settings › Apps › Night-loop › ⋮ › **Allow restricted settings**, then try again.

## Good to know

- This is a preview signed with a temporary key. Before installing the first full release you'll need to uninstall this one.
- Needs Android 8.0 or newer.
