## Night-loop for Android 0.8.3

- **Average colors box is back, next to Brightness range %.** With Match colors on, ticking "Average colors" lets any dimmer or brighter copy of the picture match. Unticked, Brightness range % says how much dimmer or brighter it may be (blank = 3, and a little screen noise is always allowed).
- **Brightness range works when the picture is found at another size.** A dimmer copy was often found a little too small, so the check only saw its brighter middle and let copies 20% dimmer through. The brightness is now measured where the colors line up best.
