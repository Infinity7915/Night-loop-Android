## Night-loop for Android 0.5.4

- **The first tap of a macro lands.** An open step card kept the keyboard focus, so the game used the first tap just to take focus back. Play now closes it first.
- **One tap opens the keyboard.** Boxes in the bubble editor no longer need a second tap, and the keyboard opens over the game.
- **Each value is edited on its own.** The keyboard key says Done and closes, instead of jumping to the next box.
- **Bubble switches:** tap the bubble for "Hide dots while playing" and "Hide play bar while playing". With the play bar hidden, stop a macro from the bubble.
