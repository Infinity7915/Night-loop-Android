Make macros right on top of your game, from the bubble.

## What's new

- **Bubble editor with dots**: tap the bubble and pick a macro. Its numbered dots appear on the screen. Tap **+ New macro** to start a fresh one.
  - **+** adds a dot and **−** removes the selected one (or the last one). Drag a dot to where the tap should land.
  - Tap a dot to set it up: **Click** (1–3 taps, how long each press lasts) or **Hold** (press, hold for a set time, release). Also set how long to wait before the next dot.
  - Dots play in number order. The label shows **H** for a hold and **×2** or **×3** for double and triple taps.
  - **▶** tests the macro. The dots step aside while it plays.
  - **⚙** sets the name and how many times it plays (blank = Infinite).
  - **✓** saves and closes. Drag the toolbar by its title if it covers your game.
- **Only Android macros** show in the bubble. PC macros can't play on a phone.
- **Auto-update**: Night-loop checks for a new version when it opens (and from **About › Check for updates**). It downloads the update and Android asks you to confirm.
- **Fits every screen**: buttons are no longer cut off, the title no longer hides under the status bar, and on tablets or sideways phones the page stays a readable width.
- The app's own editor is still there: tap an Android macro in the list to edit its blocks.

## Installing

1. **Uninstall Night-loop 0.2.0 first.** This preview is still signed with a temporary key.
2. Download `Night-loop-0.3.0.apk` below on your phone and open it.
3. Once Night-loop has its permanent signing key, updates install over the old version, and the new auto-update does it for you.

## Good to know

- Macros with loops, Open app or other blocks open in the app's editor, not as dots.
- Needs Android 8.0 or newer.
