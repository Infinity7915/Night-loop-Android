## Night-loop for Android 0.8.6

- **Plain pictures are refused.** A picture that's all one color (like part of Roblox's white loading screen) matched any plain screen of that color, so Wait for picture went on while the game was still loading, and the Type step after it found no text box. Picking one now says to pick something with detail, and a macro that has one stops and says to pick it again.
- **The picture is shown in the step's settings**, so you can see what it looks for.
- **"Is the app open" is right after typing.** The keyboard and the notification shade counted as the app in front, so "If Roblox is not open" was true while Roblox was open, and Open app waited for Roblox even though it was already in front.
