Steps stay on screen while a macro plays, plus Wait and Key steps.

## What's new

- **Steps stay on screen while playing.** They turn see-through, taps pass through them to the game, and the step that's playing lights up green. ▶ in the toolbar turns into ■ to stop. Playing from the bubble's list shows the steps too.
- **Wait step (⏱)**: pauses for as long as you set (1000 ms = 1 second). It's a chip with its number that you can park anywhere on the screen.
- **Key step (⌨)**: type text, press a key, hold a key down, or release it. Keys go to the text box that's selected, like a keyboard (Android 13 or newer). Android doesn't let apps press keys in a game that has no text box selected.
- New steps go right after the selected step, so you can slot one into the middle.
- **Renaming works.** Before, the name changed and then the editor's autosave put the old name back. Rename with ⚙ in the bubble or ✎ in the app; Save keeps it.
- **The first tap no longer gets lost.** A macro now starts 0.7 s after you press Play, once the bubble panel and Night-loop's screens are out of the way.

## Installing

1. Uninstall the older Night-loop first (this preview still uses a temporary signing key).
2. Download `Night-loop-0.4.0.apk` below on your phone and open it.
3. Turn the Night-loop accessibility service off and on again once, so Android picks up the new typing ability.

## Good to know

- Needs Android 8.0 or newer; typing and keys need Android 13 or newer.
