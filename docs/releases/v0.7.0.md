## Night-loop for Android 0.7.0

- **New block: Swipe and hold** (Add block › Touch). It swipes, then keeps your finger down at the end, like holding a joystick, so your character keeps walking. It can hold:
  - for a set time, or
  - until a check is true: text or a picture shows up on screen, text or a picture goes away, an app comes to the front, or a spot turns a color. You can also set a timeout so it lets go anyway.
- **Delete macros.** Tap 🗑 on a macro on the home page, or press and hold the macro. You can also tap 🗑 at the top of the editor. Night-loop asks before deleting.
- **Easy-to-tell swipe dots.** A swipe's start dot is green with ▶, and its end dot is red with ■. On Swipe and hold, the end dot shows ✋, because the finger stays down there.
