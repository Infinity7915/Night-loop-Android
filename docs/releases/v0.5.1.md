Create a Night-loop account right in the Android app.

## What's new

- **Create an account** on the sign-in screen: email, password (at least 12 characters, twice), agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy (both open from the page), then the 6-digit code we email you. No PC needed.

## Installing

Night-loop updates itself from Settings › Check for updates. From 0.5.0 on, updates install over the app you have.
